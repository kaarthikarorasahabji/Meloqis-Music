Meloqis Music is an expressive Android music player built around fluid playback, personal libraries, and a signature optional capsule.

Highlights:
* Seamless playback with queue, repeat, shuffle, and audio controls.
* Real-time synchronized lyrics with multiple animation styles.
* Meloqis Find for identifying music playing around you.
* Smart recommendations shaped by your listening history.
* Meloqis Brain, an on-device engine that keeps queues flowing.
* An optional animated capsule with playback controls on supported devices.
* Local privacy controls and clear telemetry opt-out choices.

Meloqis is developed with ❤️ by Kaarthik Dass Arora.
