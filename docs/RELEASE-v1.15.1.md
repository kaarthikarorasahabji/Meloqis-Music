# Meloqis Music 1.15.1

- Listen again shows recent Meloqis plays as soon as local history loads. Connected YouTube Music history joins the same grid in the background, with duplicate songs removed.
- Song artwork colours the app. Choose **Song artwork** under **Settings → Theme color**.
- Add **Meloqis Now Playing** from your Android home screen’s widget picker. The widget shows the track and artwork and gives you playback controls.
- Meloqis accepts Android media-search and playback commands. Try “Hey Google, play Faded on Meloqis.” For Bixby, open Meloqis and try “pause” or “next song.” Named-song routing depends on the assistant’s music provider support.

This release is based on v1.14.1 and can be installed over both v1.14.1 and v1.15.0.
