Meet Edge lighting, your music's new surroundings.

- New **Settings → Edge lighting** menu, separate from Equalizer, with a first-use walkthrough and a Guide button.
- Rounded edge frames with Ribbon, Comet and Pulse effects, plus Bars, Wave, Orbit and Dots.
- Four quick presets, seven colour options, album-art colours and custom two-hue gradients. Tune glow, width, speed and response.
- Frame, top, bottom, left, right or center placement, with corner, inset and camera-gap controls.
- Digital, Stacked, Analog and Minimal clock faces, optional battery and song details, adjustable position and brightness.
- Android screensaver integration for supported charging/docked setups. Select Meloqis Music Lights in Android's screensaver settings.
- A full-display, touch-through floating layer, a visible Stop control, battery-saver handling and 15–120 minute session limits.
- Existing lighting preferences are retained. Air Controls stay removed, and no festival section is added to Settings.

Real visualizers follow Meloqis playback on this device. Other music apps and Cast audio are not captured. Demo is labelled and affects only the preview. Ambient mode is decorative between songs.

The screensaver and clock display keep screen pixels active; they do not replace your phone's low-power hardware AOD. Android screensaver availability and activation vary by device. Floating lights close when the screen turns off. Use lower brightness and shorter sessions for comfort and battery life; pixel shifting cannot guarantee protection against OLED burn-in.

Download the APK matching your device or choose universal. Android 8.0 and newer are supported. Existing compatible sideload installations can update using the retained release signing identity.
