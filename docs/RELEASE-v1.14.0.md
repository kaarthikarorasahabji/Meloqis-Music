Meloqis Music 1.14.0 — Listen Again

- Home puts recently played artwork up front. Swipe through Listen again, tap a cover to play it, or queue the shelf with Play all.
- YouTube Music history appears when your connected account makes it available. Meloqis labels the source and shows this device’s listening history as a fallback.
- Mood filters stay within reach. Compact song shelves include three-dot menus for playback and queue actions.
- In the standard Android layout, the mini-player and navigation share one dock. The Liquid Glass option is available in Settings.
- Privacy and support links now open on the Meloqis website.
