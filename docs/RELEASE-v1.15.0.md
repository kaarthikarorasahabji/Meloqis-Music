Meloqis Music 1.15.0 — For You & Glass

- For you shows personal mixes from your connected YouTube Music account. Sign-in is optional.
- Listen again brings together tracks played in Meloqis and recent YouTube Music history. Repeated track IDs appear once.
- Save a mood for a song and open Browse mix to explore that mood.
- Create a playlist from Home and add the current track when you create it.
- Song artwork drives the default colour theme. Liquid Glass is enabled by default on supported Android devices and follows the saved preference and reduced-effects settings.
- The Home account menu gives you access to optional YouTube Music and Spotify connections.
