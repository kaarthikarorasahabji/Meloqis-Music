Your music, with a little more light.

- Three-dot song menus now appear while typing in Search, with Play next, queue and other song options.
- Albums have separate Play next and Add to queue actions. Play next inserts the entire album in its original order.
- New Music Lights: a live audio visualizer with Bars, Wave, Orbit and Dots modes; top, bottom, left, right, frame and center placement; four color palettes; brightness, response and size controls.
- Open an immersive display or enable the optional, touch-through floating overlay over other apps. Audio stays on your device; no microphone permission is required.
- Optional festival notifications and animated greetings, with the November wishes scheduled for 4:00 AM India time. Enable them in Settings → Little celebrations.
- Updated Listening Studio interface. Camera-based Air Controls have been removed, including their camera permission and hand-detection download.

Download the APK for your phone's architecture, or choose the universal APK. All downloads support Android 8.0 and newer. The existing release signing identity is retained so compatible installations can update in place.

Music Lights visualizes local Meloqis playback, including while other apps are open. It does not modify your phone manufacturer's hardware always-on display or capture audio from other apps. The floating display requires Android's display-over-other-apps permission and closes when the screen turns off, after inactivity or after a 30-minute session.

Festival delivery needs the new version, opt-in and notification permission. Android, network conditions and background scheduling can delay receipt. The three announced dates are 8, 9 and 10 November 2026; future festivals can be added through Meloqis Studio.
