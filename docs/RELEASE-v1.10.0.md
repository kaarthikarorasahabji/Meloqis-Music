Meloqis Music 1.10.0 — Your glow stays in Meloqis

- Edge lighting is on by default inside Meloqis, with a master switch that remembers your choice.
- Lighting follows the app's screens and stops when Meloqis goes into the background. The decorative layer does not receive touch input.
- Removed floating lights over other apps, the Android screensaver, the standalone clock display, their background service and their extra permissions.
- Removed Centre placement. Older Centre settings move to Frame; the older Orbit look becomes Twin trails along the edges.
- Seven refined edge effects: Flow, Comet, Breath, Twin trails, Bars, Wave and Sparkles. Trails wrap smoothly, glows use GPU-compatible layered strokes, and music effects stay near the border.
- Updated studio, presets, demo preview, colour palettes, edge fitting, intensity, speed and music-response controls.
- Device animation preferences are respected. Animation can be turned off, battery saver reduces the refresh rate, and lighting does not keep the screen awake.

Open Settings → Edge lighting to customize or turn it off. Equalizer remains for audio controls.

Required-update support continues for direct APK installations running 1.9.0 or later. Android asks the user to approve installation. The first Play Store submission is still pending; Play minimum-version enforcement remains disabled until an approved release is actually available there.
