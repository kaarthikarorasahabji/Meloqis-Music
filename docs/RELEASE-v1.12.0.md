Meloqis Music 1.12.0 — Surprise Me

- A compact Home shortcut builds and plays a personal mix of familiar favourites and related discoveries.
- Choose Comfort, Balanced or Adventurous to adjust how much you want to explore. Tap Surprise again for another selection.
- Mixes avoid duplicate songs, prefer songs outside recent listening and the previous mix, and vary neighbouring artists when the available pool allows.
- Downloads only makes a mix from downloaded music without asking for online recommendations. New listeners can jump straight to Search from an empty mix.
- Familiar and Discovery labels show what is in each mix. Smaller libraries and unavailable recommendations can change the balance; the app reports the actual result.
- Listening history is used only when local tracking is enabled. Likes and downloads can still seed a mix with tracking disabled. Recommendation requests happen when you request a mix, without a new account or service key.
- Cancel or leave while building a mix to keep the current queue. Existing fast streaming and bounded audio preparation remain in place.

Open Home → Surprise Me. Building recommendations can take a few seconds before playback starts. Saved favourites can fill the mix when discoveries are unavailable; only downloaded songs are available offline.

Required-update support continues for direct APK installations running 1.9.0 or later. Android asks you to approve installation. The first Play Store submission remains pending; Play minimum-version enforcement stays disabled until an approved release is available there.
