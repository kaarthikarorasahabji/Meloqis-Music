Meloqis Music 1.13.0 — Canvas & Glass

- Home opens with your current song, Canvas where available, and playback controls. Recently played songs come from local listening history when tracking is enabled.
- Liquid Glass is on by default on supported Android devices. Devices on Battery Saver or with reduced effects use the standard controls. The saved Liquid Glass preference still applies.
- Search has a steady search field, Library has a shortcut to create a playlist, and Now Playing has a larger play button.
- Surprise Me and Mix Recall have been retired. Existing playlists remain in Library.
- Automatic Edge Lighting pauses while charging. Decorative Canvas, Now Playing and Lyrics let Android apply its normal screen timeout.

Canvas follows the app’s Canvas preference and reverts to cover art when motion is unavailable. The Play bundle is ready for upload to Play Console.
