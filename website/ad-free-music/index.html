What ad-free means

An Android music player designed around listening—not impressions.

Many free apps make advertising part of the interface. Meloqis takes a different approach. It does not place display advertising between albums, interrupt navigation with pop-ups, insert sponsored recommendations into the home screen, or add audio commercials to your playback experience.

The app remains free to download, and its source code is available for inspection under the GNU GPL version 3.

Meloqis combines an uncluttered visual experience with practical Android playback: local media, synced lyrics, a flexible queue, offline listening from cached or user-owned media, and a Now Playing surface that keeps controls close.

The optional Meloqis Now Capsule can place playback controls around a centred camera cutout. It is off by default and uses Android’s “Display over other apps” permission only when you enable it.

An honest ad-free promise

Meloqis itself does not add advertisements. It is not an ad blocker and does not promise to remove advertising or sponsored content delivered by external websites, catalogs, APIs, or music providers. Those services retain their own terms and behavior.

Ad-free music app FAQ

Clear answers before you install.

Is Meloqis Music completely ad-free?+

Meloqis does not add its own banner, pop-up, sponsored-tile, or audio advertisements. External providers can still supply content under their own terms, and Meloqis does not claim to block ads from other services.

Is this ad-free Android music player free to download?+

Yes. The current Meloqis Android development preview is available as a free direct APK download from this website.

Does Meloqis work with local music files?+

Yes. Local and user-owned media are part of the Meloqis listening experience, including offline use when the files are available on your device.

Which Android versions does Meloqis support?+

The APK supports Android 8.0 and later. The optional Now Capsule is being specifically tested on Android 13 with a Samsung Galaxy S21 Ultra.

Is Meloqis open source?+

Yes. The Meloqis source code, license, notices, and modification history are available publicly on GitHub under GPLv3.

Does Meloqis block ads inside other apps?+

No. Meloqis is an ad-free music player, not a system-wide ad blocker. It controls only its own interface and playback experience.
