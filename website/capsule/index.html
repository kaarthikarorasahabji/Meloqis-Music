Meloqis Now Capsule

A small signal with full control.

01

Choose it

Open Settings → Player → Meloqis Now Capsule. It is disabled on every clean installation.

02

Allow it

Android opens the system “Display over other apps” page. Meloqis enables the feature only after you grant permission.

03

Move through music

Swipe left for the next track or right for the previous one. The artwork, title, waveform, halo, and edge colour animate into the new song.

04

Take full control

Tap to expand, drag the timeline to seek, double-tap to play or pause, swipe up to collapse, or long-press to open Meloqis.

05

Use the lock screen

Android’s media session provides artwork and playback controls on the lock screen. Samsung always-on display visibility follows the phone’s notification and media settings.

06

Remove it

Turn the setting off or revoke permission. The playback overlay is removed immediately.

Designed for your S21 Ultra.

The first device target is Android 13 on the Samsung Galaxy S21 Ultra, using its centred camera cutout as the visual anchor.

Meloqis does not use the “Dynamic Island” product name, notification access, or Android AccessibilityService for this feature.
