Privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026

Meloqis Music is an Android music app maintained by Kaarthik Dass Arora. For privacy questions or requests, email kaarthikdassarorasahabji@gmail.com.

Data on your device

The app stores settings, searches, playlists, listening history, cached media and downloads on your device. Connected-service login cookies, tokens and optional API keys may also be stored locally to provide features you enable. Android device settings, uninstalling the app, or the app's own library and account controls can remove local data. Exported backups and files saved outside app storage must be removed separately.

Connected music and other services

Search, streaming, artwork, lyrics, recommendations and account features make network requests to third-party services. Depending on what you use, these include YouTube/Google, Spotify, lyrics providers, Last.fm, Discord, GitHub and AutoEq. Requests can include search text, track identifiers, account credentials or tokens, playback activity and your IP address. Enabled scrobbling or presence features can send listening activity to your connected account. Those services apply their own privacy policies. Meloqis is independent of these services.

Camera and Air Controls

Air Controls are optional and request camera permission when enabled. Front-camera frames are processed on your device for hand gestures; frames are not saved, recorded or uploaded by this feature. First use downloads a hand recognition model from Google. If you enable background gestures, the camera can remain active through a foreground service and ongoing notification. Stop the feature in the app or notification, or revoke camera permission in Android settings.

Music Lights

The optional visualizer analyzes decoded audio playing inside Meloqis on your device. It does not use your microphone, record audio, upload audio samples or capture other apps' audio. The floating display needs Android's display-over-other-apps permission. It does not intercept touches and stops when the screen turns off, after inactivity, or after a 30-minute session. The immersive display can keep the screen awake while open.

Optional festival wishes

If you opt in, Firebase Cloud Messaging registers this app installation and subscribes it to the Meloqis festival topic in our Firebase project, EmpMetria. Firebase processes an installation registration token and related delivery metadata to send notifications. The token is not displayed in the admin panel. Dated greeting messages are stored locally so the app can show an animated wish after you open a notification. Turn festival wishes off in Settings to unsubscribe and stop the app from displaying future festival messages. Delivery can be delayed by connectivity and Android power saving.

Diagnostics and analytics

This build includes Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. These SDKs can collect app interactions, device and app information, app-instance identifiers, crash reports and diagnostics. The Play build disables advertising ID collection and advertising personalization signals. Firebase retention, processing and any configured analytics integrations depend on the Firebase project settings. See Firebase privacy information.

Optional AI features

If you enable AI features, related text such as lyrics and prompts is sent to the provider you select, using the API credentials or endpoint you configure. Provider policies and retention apply. Do not provide sensitive information in these requests.

Support, retention and deletion

If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond and investigate the issue. Support correspondence is retained while needed for the request and any applicable obligations. Do not email passwords, cookies, tokens or signing keys. For deletion instructions and requests, see Delete app data. Third-party account data must also be managed through the relevant service.

Security and choices

Restrict permissions in Android settings and disconnect services you no longer use. Network services and user-configured endpoints have their own security practices; no absolute security guarantee is made. The app is not directed at children. Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
