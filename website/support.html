Listen again

Open Home to see songs you played in Meloqis. Connect YouTube Music to include its listening history in the same grid. Meloqis shows its own history first and combines the two sources when the connection finishes loading.

Home screen widget

Long-press a blank space on your Android home screen, open Widgets, and add Meloqis Now Playing. The widget shows the track, artist and artwork and lets you play, pause or skip.

Voice playback

Try “Hey Google, play Faded on Meloqis.” On a Galaxy device, try “pause” or “next song” with Meloqis open. Your assistant determines which apps it can open to play a named song.

Edge lighting

Open Settings → Edge lighting to change the effect, colours or placement, or switch it off. The lights run inside Meloqis and pause while charging. Tap Guide in Edge lighting for the controls. Sound adjustments are under Audio → Equalizer.

Library and accounts

Back up your library before clearing app storage. Disconnect optional music-service accounts in the app and manage service permissions on the provider's website.
