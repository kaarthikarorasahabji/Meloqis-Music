Edge lighting and clock displays

In version 1.8 and later, open Settings → Edge lighting. Sound adjustments remain under Audio → Equalizer. Tap Guide at the top of Edge lighting to reopen the walkthrough.

  1. Pick a preset in Design, then choose an effect and colour palette. Demo animates only the preview; real displays use Meloqis playback on this device.
  2. Use Fit to choose an edge or the whole frame, adjust rounded corners and leave space for your camera cutout.
  3. Open clock display for a black-background music display. Choose a clock, battery information, brightness and song details in Clock.
  4. To show lights over other apps, enable Android's display-over-other-apps permission when prompted. Stop from Edge lighting or the ongoing notification.
  5. For charging or docked use, open Set up Android screensaver and select Meloqis Music Lights. Phone support and Android settings determine when it starts.

Floating lights stop on screen-off, session expiry or removal from Recents. Ambient glow can remain between songs; turn it off to stop after a minute without local audio. Choose a 15–120 minute session and battery-saver behavior in Behavior. The Android screensaver exits on touch and follows the system's charging/docking rules.

These are screen-on displays, not a replacement for the phone's low-power hardware AOD. Other players and Cast audio do not drive the visualizer. Use low brightness and shorter sessions; content shifting does not guarantee protection against OLED burn-in.

Library and accounts
