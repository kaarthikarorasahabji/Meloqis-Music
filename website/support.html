Listen again

Recent tracks played in Meloqis appear under Listen again. Connect YouTube Music to include your account history and see your personal mixes on For you. Open the account icon on Home to manage YouTube Music or Spotify. Both connections are optional.

Song colours, moods and playlists

Open Settings → User interface to choose Song artwork as the theme colour or change the Liquid Glass setting. On Home, use Song mood to save a mood for the current track and Browse mix to explore it. The playlist button lets you name a new playlist and include the song that is playing.

Edge lighting

Open Settings → Edge lighting to change the effect, colours or placement, or switch it off. The lights run inside Meloqis and pause while charging. Tap Guide in Edge lighting for the controls. Sound adjustments are under Audio → Equalizer.

Library and accounts

Back up your library before clearing app storage. Disconnect optional music-service accounts in the app and manage service permissions on the provider's website.
