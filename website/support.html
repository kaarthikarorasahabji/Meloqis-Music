Listen again

Open Home and tap Connect YouTube Music to browse recent listening from your connected account. Meloqis shows recent tracks from this device when account history is unavailable. The label under Listen again shows the source.

Edge lighting

Open Settings → Edge lighting to change the effect, colours or placement, or switch it off. The lights run inside Meloqis and pause while charging. Tap Guide in Edge lighting for the controls. Sound adjustments are under Audio → Equalizer.

Library and accounts

Back up your library before clearing app storage. Disconnect optional music-service accounts in the app and manage service permissions on the provider's website.
