Permission with a purpose

Optional means optional.

The Now Capsule asks for Android’s “Display over other apps” permission only when you turn it on. Disable it and the overlay disappears.

Questions

Straight answers

Is Meloqis really an ad-free music app?+

Yes. Meloqis does not add banner ads, pop-ups, sponsored recommendations, or audio advertisements to its interface. External music providers can still have their own content, rules, and service terms.

Is Meloqis available now?+

Yes. The free Android development preview can be downloaded directly from the Meloqis website. It is debug-signed and intended for early testing.

Will the Now Capsule work on Android 13?+

Yes. It is specifically designed around Android’s overlay permission, with the Samsung Galaxy S21 Ultra as the first target device.

Does the capsule read other apps?+

No. It displays only Meloqis playback state and does not use notification access or an accessibility service.

Is Meloqis open source?+

Yes. Meloqis is a modified GPLv3 fork, and its source code, notices, and modification history are published on GitHub.

Who develops Meloqis?+

Meloqis is developed by Kaarthik Dass Arora through Axenora AI.
