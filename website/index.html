Before you press play

A few good answers.

Which version should I download?

There is just one download button. It always opens the latest universal APK, including the supported phone architectures. Meloqis requires Android 8.0 or above.

How do I update my app?

Download the latest update above, then confirm installation on Android. Install over your existing Meloqis app to keep your library and settings. You can also check for updates in the app’s Settings.

Can I listen offline?

Download supported songs in the app for offline playback. Streaming, search, and lyric availability depend on the connected music services.

How do the queue gestures work?

In song lists, hold a row and move right to Play Next. Hold and release for queue actions. In the queue, hold and move vertically to reorder. Playlist reorder mode uses that gesture for playlist ordering.

Do I need to support the developer?

Support is optional. The app’s reminder appears at most once a week. The coffee button opens a ₹50 UPI request for you to confirm in your payment app.
