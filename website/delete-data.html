Meloqis Music · Privacy · Support

Delete app data

Remove data from your phone

  1. Export anything you want to keep.
  2. Sign out of connected accounts in Meloqis Music.
  3. Open Android Settings → Apps → Meloqis Music → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  4. Delete any exported backups and downloads saved outside the app's storage using your files app.

Clearing storage removes local settings, library records and login sessions. It cannot remove copies you exported or data already held by third-party providers.

Connected accounts

Meloqis Music does not create a separate Meloqis login account in this version. YouTube/Google, Spotify, Last.fm, Discord and AI accounts belong to their providers. Revoke access and request deletion from those providers as needed. Deleting Meloqis does not delete a third-party account.

Ask the developer to delete data

Email kaarthikdassarorasahabji@gmail.com with the subject “Meloqis Music data deletion”. Describe which support correspondence or other developer-held information you want removed. You may be asked for information needed to verify the request; never send passwords or login tokens. Legal obligations may require retaining some records, which will be explained in the response.

Firebase diagnostic and analytics records are subject to the project's retention and deletion capabilities. Include approximate dates and relevant crash details if requesting help identifying a record; the developer may not be able to link anonymous diagnostic records to an individual.
